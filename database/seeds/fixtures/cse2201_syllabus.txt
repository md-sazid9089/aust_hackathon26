CSE 2201 Design and Analysis of Algorithms
Credits: 3.0 | Prerequisite: CSE 2101 Data Structures

Course Outcomes
CO1 Analyse the asymptotic time and space complexity of iterative and recursive algorithms.
CO2 Apply divide-and-conquer and comparison-based sorting algorithms to solve problems.
CO3 Design dynamic-programming solutions for optimisation problems and justify optimal substructure.
CO4 Apply greedy algorithms and evaluate the correctness of greedy choices.
CO5 Apply graph algorithms (BFS, DFS, shortest paths, minimum spanning trees) to model and solve problems.
CO6 Explain the classes P, NP and NP-complete and describe polynomial-time reductions.

Topics
T01 Asymptotic notation: Big-O, Big-Omega, Big-Theta; growth of functions
T02 Recurrence relations, substitution method, recursion tree, Master theorem
T03 Divide and conquer: binary search, maximum subarray, Strassen's matrix multiplication
T04 Sorting: merge sort, quicksort, heap sort, lower bound for comparison sorting
T05 Dynamic programming: rod cutting, 0/1 knapsack, longest common subsequence, matrix-chain
T06 Greedy algorithms: activity selection, fractional knapsack, Huffman coding
T07 Graph traversal: BFS, DFS, topological sort, strongly connected components
T08 Shortest paths: Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford, Floyd-Warshall
T09 Minimum spanning trees: Prim's and Kruskal's algorithms, union-find
T10 NP-completeness: P vs NP, reductions, Vertex Cover, SAT, Hamiltonian cycle

Assessment: Midterm 20%, Final 50%, Quizzes 10%, Assignments 20%
