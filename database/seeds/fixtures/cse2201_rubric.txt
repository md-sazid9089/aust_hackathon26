Rubric: Written algorithm-analysis answer (max 20)

C1 Correctness (max 5)
  5 Excellent: result and reasoning fully correct
  3 Adequate: minor slip that does not change the conclusion
  1 Weak: major error or unsupported claim

C2 Completeness (max 5)
  5 Excellent: all required steps and cases covered
  3 Adequate: one required step missing
  1 Weak: most steps missing

C3 Clarity (max 5)
  5 Excellent: well structured, precise notation
  3 Adequate: readable but loosely organised
  1 Weak: hard to follow

C4 Complexity analysis (max 5)
  5 Excellent: tight bound derived and justified
  3 Adequate: correct bound stated without derivation
  1 Weak: bound missing or wrong
